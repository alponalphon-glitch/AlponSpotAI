# NEXORA Central Brain MAX — additive layer

This ZIP adds a five-part strategic diagnostic report to the existing decision report:
- Liquidity Intelligence: candle/market-structure level proxy only (not live order-book liquidity).
- Event Impact: reports available news context and explicitly states that no macro calendar or causal impact model is connected.
- Analyst Council: summarizes existing rule-based evidence modules; it does not claim separate trained AI agents.
- Execution Reality: remains unavailable until spread, fee, slippage, order-size, and depth inputs are wired in.
- Market Knowledge Graph: descriptive links among currently available market context values, not causal inference.

The new `CentralBrainStrategicLayer.kt` is additive. Existing files were backed up before editing, and no original ZIP entries were removed. GitHub was not contacted and no push/build was performed.

## Final central-brain quality layer (2026-11-23)
- Decision Trace: compact trace of the synthesized direction, evidence count, integrity, conflicts, blockers, and final action.
- Live Decision Monitor: current-snapshot alert state; it is explicitly not a persistent real-time monitor and must be refreshed after new data.
- Intelligence Conflict Resolver: descriptive bullish-vs-bearish analyst panel conflict check; does not replace existing decision gates.
- Evidence-Based Learning: regime-scoped closed Paper outcomes summary (sample count, win rate, average PnL); descriptive only and cannot promote a strategy or increase risk.
