# NEXORA Validation Governance — 2026-11-23

## Purpose

The central brain now reports research maturity and operational evidence separately from directional analysis. These controls do not manufacture model accuracy, forecast profits, or authorize exchange orders.

## Deep Learning and Reinforcement Learning

- Deep Learning remains a compact on-device neural research prototype. A preliminary chronological holdout pass can qualify it only as low-weight shadow evidence.
- Reinforcement Learning remains a tabular Q-learning research policy. A preliminary out-of-sample pass does not prove robustness across unseen symbols, timeframes, costs, or regimes.
- The central brain labels both model states explicitly and keeps shadow-only models out of any claim of production readiness.
- Promotion to production would require repeated walk-forward and untouched future-period validation, cost-aware results, stability checks, drift monitoring, and independent review. No such production promotion is implemented.

## Long-term Paper Trading validation

The governance report measures the timestamp span, number of valid closed paper outcomes, and distinct market regimes represented in the stored outcome history. The initial evidence threshold is 30 days, 100 closed trades, and 3 regimes. Until all three are met, the report remains `LONG-TERM PAPER VALIDATION INCOMPLETE`.

Meeting the threshold is only a minimum evidence checkpoint, not proof of continuous app uptime, profitability, or future performance. The current outcome schema does not prove uninterrupted paper-trading operation.

## Futures margin and liquidation

Futures remains analysis-only. A derivatives market snapshot (funding, mark/index basis, open interest, positioning) is not an account-specific liquidation calculator. Before any future execution feature could be considered, the system would need verified exchange/account values for leverage, margin mode, maintenance-margin tier, wallet equity, position notional, fees, slippage, mark price, exchange liquidation price and liquidation distance, plus validated stop/size controls. Missing or stale values must fail closed.

This upgrade deliberately does not add live Futures order placement or pretend liquidation is verified.

## Monte Carlo interpretation

Monte Carlo remains a bootstrap resampling diagnostic over recent closed Paper Trading outcomes. It reports historical-sequence stress summaries only. The result is not a calibrated probability of future drawdown, not a price forecast, and not a guarantee. Serial dependence, regime shifts, liquidity gaps, unrecorded fees, and changing execution costs can materially change outcomes.

## Execution policy

- Binance Spot: existing Paper Trading policy only; no live Binance order endpoint is authorized by this upgrade.
- Futures: analysis/signals only; execution remains disabled.
- Research models: shadow-only; cannot bypass data-integrity, risk, red-team, walk-forward, or final master gates.
- The readiness percentage is a count of subsystem evidence/status labels, not model accuracy or a profitability score.
